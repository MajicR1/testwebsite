// Overview

Unlock is a VR escape room experience built in Unity. Players are placed inside a series of interconnected puzzle rooms and must use their hands, environment, and wits to find a way out before time runs out.

I build this project while working at an internship in 2023 hosted by the Georgia Institute of Technology. This was my first introduction to programming, and game design. All of the puzzles were based on STEM fields. I personally worked to create three of the puzzles in one of the rooms, which was quite a lot at the time, considering I didn't know how to code yet. Also, due to team size this you had to learn to wear many hats and so I did - C#, Blender, and Audacity. We had to make our own audio, and models for the game.
